# My Plan+ Community

A single 12 Week Year accountability club. Members run their own 12-week plans, and meet weekly in small groups that persist across seasons.

## Structure

**Club**:
The one organization (12WC) that every member, group and season belongs to.
_Avoid_: Org, community, organization

**Season**:
One 12 Week Year: twelve scored weeks plus a thirteenth break-and-reflection week, which members enrol in, plan against, and check in on weekly. Seasons are numbered (Season 24, S25).
_Avoid_: Cycle, quarter, term

**Group**:
A small, lasting accountability team with its own weekly WAM time. It outlives any single season; who is in it is recorded per season as its roster.
_Avoid_: Team, pod, squad

**Purpose**:
A group's short statement of what it focuses on or who it suits, shown to prospective members.
_Avoid_: Description, theme, interests

**Roster**:
The members of a group for one specific season, one of whom is its group commander.
_Avoid_: Membership list, group members

**WAM**:
One Weekly Accountability Meeting of a single group: one occurrence of that group's recurring weekly meeting within a season. Groups meeting at the same time still hold separate WAMs.
_Avoid_: Session, event, activity, meeting

## Group health

**Minimum Size**:
The smallest roster at which a group can run properly: club-wide (2) unless a club manager or that group's commander overrides it for that group.
_Avoid_: Floor, minimum membership

**Capacity**:
The largest roster a group aims to hold: club-wide (4) unless a club manager overrides it for that group.
_Avoid_: Max size, typical size, limit

**Vacancy**:
An unfilled place on a group's roster, below its capacity.
_Avoid_: Open slot, opening, spot

**Forming**:
A group running this season that is below minimum size or has no group commander.
_Avoid_: Incomplete, pending

**Active**:
A group running this season at or above minimum size, with a group commander.
_Avoid_: Healthy, live

**Dormant**:
A group a club manager has set as not running this season, kept for history and possible revival.
_Avoid_: Inactive, deactivated, archived, deleted

**Health Flag**:
A specific, named reason a group needs attention, such as no group commander, a vacancy, low attendance or an open transfer request.
_Avoid_: Health score, alert, warning

**At Risk**:
A group with at least one health flag.
_Avoid_: Unhealthy, red, failing

## Roles

**Member**:
A registered person in the club who runs their own 12-week plan and can be placed on a group's roster.
_Avoid_: User, participant, registrant

**Unplaced Member**:
A member enrolled for a season who is not yet on any group's roster for it.
_Avoid_: Unassigned, available member, free agent

**Club Manager**:
A person responsible for running the whole club: its seasons, groups and member pool.
_Avoid_: Admin, organiser

**Group Commander**:
The member on a group's roster who is responsible for operating that group for the season: its roster, WAMs and communication.
_Avoid_: Leader, facilitator, captain, host

## Placement

**Invite Link**:
A member's personal, shareable link, with a pre-written message, inviting someone to register and join the club; it brings them in as an unplaced member.
_Avoid_: Referral, invitation (that is a group commander's offer to a member)

**Pending Member**:
A person added to a group's roster by name and email before they have registered; they hold a place and become a member when they sign up with that email.
_Avoid_: Placeholder, ghost member, pre-registration

**Enrolment**:
The period before a season starts, often overlapping the previous season, when members confirm continuation and unplaced members are placed into groups.
_Avoid_: Sign-up, registration

**Preferences**:
What a member states to guide placement: the weekly times they can attend, one preferred time, and what they are looking for in a group.
_Avoid_: Profile, settings, criteria

**Continuation**:
A member's confirmation, during enrolment, that they will stay on their group's roster for the next season.
_Avoid_: Re-enrolment, renewal, carry-over

**Invitation**:
An offer from a group commander to a member to join their group's roster, which the member accepts or declines.
_Avoid_: Invite request, offer

**Join Request**:
A member's request to join a specific group, which that group's commander approves or declines.
_Avoid_: Application, self-join

**Assignment**:
A club manager placing a member directly onto a group's roster.
_Avoid_: Placement override, manual placement

**Allocation**:
The automatic grouping of members left unplaced at the end of enrolment, based on shared availability.
_Avoid_: Random assignment, auto-matching

**Leaving**:
A member removing themselves from their group's roster mid-season, which takes effect immediately and leaves them unplaced.
_Avoid_: Dropping out, quitting, removal

**Departure**:
A group commander recording that a member has left their roster, with a reason; it frees the vacancy at once and a club manager can reverse it.
_Avoid_: Removal, kick, eviction

**Suggested Match**:
A pairing of an unplaced member and a group with a vacancy whose WAM time the member can attend, shown with its plain-language reason; a person always makes the final choice.
_Avoid_: Recommendation, auto-match, score

**Transfer Request**:
A member's request to a club manager to move to a different group; they stay on their current roster until assigned elsewhere.
_Avoid_: Reassignment request, move request

## Participation

**RSVP**:
A member's stated intention for one WAM. Every member is presumed attending unless they say they can't make it.
_Avoid_: Response, availability (for a single WAM)

**Attendance**:
The record of who actually took part in a WAM, confirmed by the group commander and defaulting to the RSVPs.
_Avoid_: Check-in (that means the weekly score)

**Check-in**:
A member's weekly self-assessment against their 12-week plan: a percentage score plus answers to the WAM questions.
_Avoid_: Score entry, report, attendance

**Cancelled WAM**:
A single WAM the group commander has called off; it does not count against attendance, but that week's check-ins still apply.
_Avoid_: Skipped week, break week

**Pulse**:
A member's private, lightweight rating of how well their group is working for them, collected in weeks 4, 8 and 13.
_Avoid_: Survey, feedback form, satisfaction score

**On-behalf Entry**:
A check-in, RSVP or preferences change that a group commander or club manager records for a member, marked with who entered it; the member can always overwrite it.
_Avoid_: Proxy, override

## Communication

**Notice**:
An item in a member's in-app inbox telling them something happened and what action, if any, they need to take.
_Avoid_: Notification, alert, message

**Reminder**:
An action a member, group commander or club manager still owes, such as a missing check-in or unconfirmed attendance; it stays open until the action is done and cannot be dismissed or marked read.
_Avoid_: Nudge, to-do, task, digest

**Announcement**:
A one-way message from a club manager or group commander to a defined audience, delivered as a notice, with its audience recorded.
_Avoid_: Broadcast, blast, post (a post belongs on a message board)

**Message Board**:
A discussion space, either private to one group or open to the whole club, where members post and reply.
_Avoid_: Forum, chat, channel
